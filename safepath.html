Gary took a safe path

Gary has followed a safe path so far, carefully stepping where he was guided and avoiding the hidden dangers beneath the ground. Each move has brought him a little farther from the deadly mines, and for now he remains unharmed. However, he is not yet out of the minefield, and the threat still surrounds him. One wrong step could change everything, so he must continue forward with caution and careful guidance.
